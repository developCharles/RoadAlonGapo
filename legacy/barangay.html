Verification Guidelines

Official Verification Protocols

Follow these guidelines to ensure accurate and consistent road hazard verification across Olongapo City.

Step-by-Step Process
1
Review Evidence Photo

Ensure the uploaded photo is clear, recent, and clearly shows the reported hazard. Blurry or irrelevant photos should lead to rejection.

2
Verify Location on Map

Cross-check the GPS coordinates with the barangay map. Confirm the location falls within Olongapo City's jurisdiction.

3
Check for Duplicates

Before verifying, check if an identical report for the same location has already been submitted. Reject duplicates to maintain a clean queue.

4
Assign Maintenance Team

Select the appropriate team based on hazard type. Use Emergency Response Team for urgent/life-threatening hazards, Flood Control Unit for flooding.

✓
Submit Verification

Once all checks are complete, tap "Verify Hazard." The report is automatically forwarded to City Engineering Office for resolution.

When to Reject

✕ Photo is unrelated to the description
✕ Location does not match Olongapo boundaries
✕ Report is clearly a duplicate
✕ Description is incomplete or misleading
✕ No actual hazard visible in evidence

Help & Support
FAQ
How long do I have to review a report?
Aim for under 2 hours. Reports older than 24 hours are automatically flagged as delayed and escalated to supervisors.
Can I undo a verification?
No. Once a report is verified or rejected, it is logged permanently. Contact your supervisor to reverse an action.
Who handles reports after I verify them?
Verified reports are sent to the City Engineering Office queue for field resolution and repair.
How do I report a system issue?
Email the technical team at support@roadalongapo.gov.ph or call the City IT helpline at (047) 224-0000.
